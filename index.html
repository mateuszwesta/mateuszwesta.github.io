About

I sit between research and delivery: turning ambitious AI and audio programs into shipped products. My background spans text-to-speech engineering, UX research, and technical program management, which means I can hold a technical conversation with the people building the system and still keep a release on schedule.

Before program management I owned the end-to-end pipeline for new TTS voices at Amazon, and earlier built the Polish Google Voice from text normalization through to release. I have worked across Mountain View, New York, London, Tokyo, and Gdańsk.
